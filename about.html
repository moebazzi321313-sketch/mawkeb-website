About Us

We are Mawkeb Saheb al-Zaman, a community rooted in faith and service

Mawkeb Saheb al-Zaman, also known as Themawkeb or the Mawkeb, is a Shia community organisation in the Detroit area. We gather in remembrance, learn together and give our time to serving our community.

Our work includes the Sayyida Fatima (Fatimiyya) program, a year-round Activities unit, lessons and campaigns with The Ashura Project.
