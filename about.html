About the Mawkeb

Mawkeb Saheb al-Zaman, also known as Themawkeb or simply the Mawkeb, is a Shia community organisation in the Detroit area.

Faith, learning and service

We gather in remembrance of Ahl al-Bayt (AS), learn together, and give our time to serving our community.

Our work includes Abna Al-Batoul in remembrance of Lady Fatima al-Zahra (AS), Layali Al-Qadr, the Madheef, a year-round Activities unit, lessons, and campaigns with The Ashura Project.

Saheb al-Zaman, the Master of the Age, is a title of the twelfth Imam, al-Mahdi (AJ). The Mawkeb carries his name.

How the Mawkeb is run

Every program is planned by nine volunteer teams, each with its own lead, plan and checklist, from the first meeting to the last box packed into storage.

  • SetupBuilds the hall before the night and breaks it down after.
  • LogisticsFlyers, the night's program, supplies and the storage room.
  • LatmThe latmiya, the reciters and the recording.
  • MadheefCooks and serves the food for everyone who attends.
  • SecurityDoors, parking and a safe hall.
  • MediaCameras, lighting and sound, and the releases afterwards.
  • SistersRuns the sisters' side of the program.
  • SalesThe Mawkeb's merchandise table.
  • CleaningLeaves the hall the way we found it.

The Activities team runs the Mawkeb's year-round activities, with its own lead.

Our story so far

The Mawkeb's archive of Abna Al-Batoul begins in 1445, and each year since has had its own emblem, stage and latmiya. You can walk the four years on the homepage trail or read them on the Abna Al-Batoul page.
