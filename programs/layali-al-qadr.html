The nights

Laylat al-Qadr, the Night of Power, is sought on the nights of the 19th, 21st and 23rd of Ramadan. The Mawkeb opens its doors on these nights for worship, remembrance and a shared meal.

This year's dates, times and venue will be announced before Ramadan. Follow the Mawkeb to hear first.

Each night includes

  • The nightly programThe order of the night, published in advance on a flyer.
  • The dua scheduleWhich supplications are read and when, also published for each night.
  • A kids programA program for children alongside the main gathering.
  • A mealCooked and served by the Madheef team for everyone attending.

Volunteering on Layali Al-Qadr

The nights need every team: Setup to prepare the hall, the Madheef team for the meals, Security for doors and parking, the Sisters team for the sisters' side, Media for the cameras, and Cleaning after.
