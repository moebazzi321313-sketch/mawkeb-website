Arbaeen

الأربعين

Arbaeen marks forty days after the martyrdom of Imam Husayn (AS). Millions walk to Karbala, and the mawakib along the road serve them.

The Mawkeb's Arbaeen

The word mawkeb is best known from Arbaeen, when mawakib line the road to Karbala to feed and shelter the visitors of Imam Husayn (AS). That spirit of service runs through everything the Mawkeb does.

The Mawkeb's Arbaeen services and activities, and photos from previous years, are being gathered and will be published here once confirmed.
