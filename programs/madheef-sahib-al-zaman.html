Hospitality in the name of the Imam

A madheef is a place of hospitality. Along the road to Karbala, mawakib feed every visitor freely, and the Mawkeb carries that tradition home: no one leaves a program night hungry.

The Madheef team plans the food for each night, cooks, serves, and packs everything away again, with its own lead, plan and supplies.
