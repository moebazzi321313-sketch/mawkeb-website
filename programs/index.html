Every program, the same nine teams

Whatever the program, the night is planned and run by the Mawkeb's nine volunteer teams, each with its own lead.

  • SetupBuilds the hall before the night and breaks it down after.
  • LogisticsFlyers, the night's program, supplies and storage.
  • LatmThe latmiya, the reciters and the recording.
  • MadheefCooks and serves the food.
  • SecurityDoors, parking and a safe hall.
  • MediaCameras, lighting, sound, and the releases afterwards.
  • SistersRuns the sisters' side of the program.
  • SalesThe merchandise table.
  • CleaningLeaves the hall the way we found it.
