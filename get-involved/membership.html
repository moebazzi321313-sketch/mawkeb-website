Become a member

Membership is how the Mawkeb knows who it serves and who can serve with it. Registration takes a few minutes on our secure form.

Register as a member

What registration asks

The form asks for your name and how to reach you, a little about you, and which parts of the Mawkeb's work you'd like to take part in. There is space for anything else you want the team to know.

Who sees your answers

Answers are kept on the Mawkeb's own website. Only Mawkeb administrators can review them, behind a sign-in, and they are used only to organise programs and volunteers.
