Five ways to support the Madheef

  • Sponsor a mealCover the food for a program night, or part of one. Use the form below and the team will tell you what that night needs.
  • Contribute foodBring or send food for a night, arranged with the team so it fits the menu and the number of guests.
  • Give moneyA gift of any amount to the Madheef, once or monthly. Give to the Madheef
  • VolunteerCook, serve and clear with the Madheef team. Join the Madheef team
  • Ask about sponsorshipFor a family, business or group sponsorship, or anything else, use the form below.

There are no fixed prices or packages: meal costs and quantities depend on the night, and the Madheef team sets them for each program.
