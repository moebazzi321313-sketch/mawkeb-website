Questions people ask

If your question is not here, send it to us and we will answer.

Who can come to a program?

Everyone. Programs are open to the community; the sisters' side is run by the Sisters team.

Where are programs held?

It depends on the program. The Sayyida Fatima program in September 2026 was at the Taha Building. Each program's venue is announced on our social media before the night.

How do I hear about the next program?

Follow the Mawkeb on social media. Dates, times and flyers are posted there first.

How can I volunteer?

Fill in the volunteer form and choose a team. Every team has a lead, and new volunteers work alongside people who have done it before.

What is the difference between volunteering and membership?

Membership registers you with the Mawkeb so we know who we serve; volunteering puts you on a team for program nights. Many people do both.

How do I donate?

On the donate page you can choose a program to support, once or monthly. Payment happens on our payment provider's secure page.

Can I give by Zelle?

Yes, once the Mawkeb's Zelle details are published on the donate page. Only send to the details shown there; Zelle gifts are confirmed by hand.

How do I sponsor a meal at the Madheef?

Use the sponsor the Madheef form. The Madheef team will contact you with what the night needs.

When does a latmiya come out after the night?

The full video is released about eight days after the night, followed by reels on day 11 and day 15, with the audio on SoundCloud and Instagram Music on day 15.

Can I use photos from this website?

Please ask first through the contact form.
