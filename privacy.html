Privacy

How the Mawkeb looks after what you send through this website.

This notice is a draft for the Mawkeb to review before the site goes live.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: for example your name, email, phone number and how you would like to help. The site does not use advertising trackers.

Why

To organise volunteers, sponsors and members, and to answer your messages. Nothing else.

Who sees it

Only the Mawkeb team members responsible for that area. We never sell or share your details.

Payments

Donations and store payments are handled by our payment provider on their own secure page. Your card details never reach this website.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what you have sent at any time through the contact form.

Photos

Photos on this site are from Mawkeb programs. If you appear in one and would like it removed, let us know and we will take it down.
